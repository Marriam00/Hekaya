Curator's Synopsis history_edu

"Books are mirrors: you only see in them what you already have inside you."

Hidden in the labyrinthine heart of Barcelona's Gothic Quarter lies the Cemetery of Forgotten Books. Ten-year-old Daniel Sempere is allowed to select one volume that changes his destiny. As he unravels the mystery of author Julián Carax, the shadows of love, curse, and totalitarian retribution follow him.
