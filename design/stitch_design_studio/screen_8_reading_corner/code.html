Revisiting Calvino’s Invisible Cities tonight. There is a line that struck me with renewed force:

“With cities, it is as with dreams: everything imaginable can be dreamed, but even the most unexpected dream is a rebus that conceals a desire...”

How many of us read a book once for the story, and the second time for the city it builds in our thoughts?
